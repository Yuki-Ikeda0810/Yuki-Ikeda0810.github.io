01 / ABOUT

Bridging intelligent robotics on Earth
and robotics in space.

This site presents key research topics, research and development projects, and research outputs.

03 / PROJECTS

Projects

JAXA

Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (JAXA) ↗ JAXA Research and Development Directorate ↗

2023–Present

DEBRIS REMOVAL

CRD2 (Commercial Removal of Debris Demonstration)

CRD2 is a JAXA project conducted with private companies to demonstrate the approach, inspection, capture, and removal of large space debris in stages. It aims to establish debris removal technologies and foster sustainable on-orbit servicing businesses. As a project member, I contribute to research and development on debris capture.

SIMULATION & DYNAMICS

SATDyn / C2Dyn

SATDyn is a ground test facility that reproduces space dynamics to verify approach and capture technologies for on-orbit servicing. In particular, we are developing C2Dyn, which specializes in simulating contact and capture dynamics that were difficult to reproduce with SATDyn.

ROBOT SOFTWARE

Space ROS

As part of the Space ROS initiative to bring ROS (Robot Operating System) to space applications, we are developing RACS (ROS and cFS System), which bridges ROS, a robotics software framework, and cFS, a spacecraft software framework. By integrating these two frameworks, we aim to use ROS software and development resources established in terrestrial robotics for space robots while maintaining safety and reliability.

ROBOT COMPETITION

RoboCup@Space

RoboCup JapanOpen @Space Challenge is a competition and benchmark built around tasks inspired by space robot operations. Starting with a simulated ISS environment, it brings together competing AI and autonomous control technologies, with the aim of expanding to future applications in orbit, on the Moon, and in planetary exploration. We work with collaborators to design and run the competition and benchmark.

FREE-FLYING ROBOT

Q — Intra-station Robot

Q is an AI-powered autonomous robot for use inside space stations. It flies freely through the cabin using built-in propellers. It was developed to support astronauts by reducing their workload and lowering the operational costs of their activities.

DIGITAL TWIN

Int-Ball2 Digital Twin

An in-orbit demonstration using JAXA’s Int-Ball2 was conducted to develop the digital twin technology needed for Q. The robot’s motion aboard the ISS (left) was compared with its motion in the simulator (right) as part of this demonstration.

2017–2023

SOBITS / SOKA UNIVERSITY

SOBIT EDU

SOBIT EDU is an AI-powered autonomous service robot. It combines object recognition and manipulation with autonomous navigation and natural communication, bringing together fundamental capabilities for helping people in their everyday lives.

SOBITS / SOKA UNIVERSITY

SOBIT MINI

SOBIT MINI is an AI-powered autonomous service robot. Developed for everyday assistance and an approachable appearance, it features carefully designed head and body shapes, along with expressive behaviors such as blinking.

SOBITS / SOKA UNIVERSITY

SOBIT PRO

SOBIT PRO is an AI-powered autonomous service robot. Addressing limitations of EDU and MINI, it combines omnidirectional mobility and an expanded grasping range with a gripper-mounted RGB-D camera for improved perception.

SOBITS / SOKA UNIVERSITY

SOBY

SOBY is an AI-powered autonomous robot with two legs and two wheels. Its charming design explores how simply being nearby, even without performing tasks, can offer companionship and help robots support people.
